GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is a brand visibility method tailored to AI search. Where SEO ranks brands in search results, GEO gets brands recommended inside AI answers. More and more users go straight to DeepSeek, Doubao, Kimi and other AI platforms for answers, and a brand&#x27;s position in those AI recommendations directly shapes user decisions. Which LLM platforms are supported? We currently support 5 leading LLM platforms—DeepSeek, Doubao, Yuanbao, Kimi, and Qwen—and continuously expand based on market adoption. All platforms are monitored daily in parallel and can be compared side by side in a single dashboard. How is the AI position score calculated? The AI position score combines Visibility Score, average rank, and citation quality. Visibility Score measures how often the brand appears in AI answers; average rank reflects the brand&#x27;s position in recommendation lists (lower is better); citation sources track which external domains and articles the AI cited. Combined, they summarize your brand&#x27;s visibility in AI search as a single number. How long until I get my first report? Once you&#x27;ve created a workspace and brand and added monitoring prompts, most teams get their first visibility report within 5 minutes. From then on, the system collects answers from every platform daily, updating Visibility Score, rank, and citation trends. How often does monitoring run? Once configured, the system automatically collects answers from every platform in parallel on a daily cadence—no manual trigger needed. Visibility Score, average rank, and citation sources update every day, and you can review trends and volatility alerts from the dashboard. Can I monitor competitors at the same time? Yes. Once you add competitors to your brand settings, you can compare Visibility Score, average rank, and citation sources side by side, and view the competitive landscape by topic group.
